## Welcome to SMS

The Student Management System is your one-stop platform for managing academic activities. Whether you're an instructor entering grades or an administrator organizing courses, SMS makes it simple.

### What You Can Do

📚 **Organize Semesters** - Set up Fall, Spring, and Summer terms  
📖 **Manage Courses** - Create and edit your course catalog  
🎓 **Track Majors** - Organize academic programs  
📊 **Enter Grades** - Use our intuitive gradebook  
✅ **Monitor Attendance** - Track student participation  
📱 **Access Anywhere** - Works on desktop, tablet, and phone

---

## Getting Started

### Login to SMS

1. Open your browser and go to your institution's SMS website
2. Enter your **username** and **password**
3. Click **"Login"**

### Your Dashboard

When you log in, you'll see:

- **Side menu** on the left with Semesters, Courses, and Majors
- **Main area** where you'll work
- **User profile** in the top corner
- **Theme toggle** to switch between light and dark mode

> **Tip:** Click the ☰ menu icon on mobile to open/close the sidebar

---

## Managing Semesters

Semesters help you organize your academic calendar - Fall, Spring, Summer, etc.

### Add a New Semester

1. Click **"Semesters"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Semester"**
3. Fill in semester name, start date, and end date
4. Click **"Save"**

### Edit a Semester

1. Find your semester in the list
2. Click the **⋯** menu → **"Edit"**
3. Make your changes and save

---

## Managing Courses

Your course catalog lives here. Browse, add, and edit all your courses.

### Add a New Course

1. Click **"Courses"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Course"**
3. Enter course code, name, credits, and description
4. Click **"Save"**

### Create Course Sections

Sections are specific instances of a course (like CS101 Section 01 for Fall 2026).

1. Click **⋯** on a course → **"View Sections"**
2. Click **"Add Section"**
3. Assign the semester, instructor, and schedule
4. Save

## Managing Majors

Organize all your academic programs in one place.

### Add a Major

1. Click **"Majors"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Major"**
3. Enter major name and details
4. Save

### Quick Edit

**Double-click** any major name to edit it quickly. Press Enter to save!

> **Mobile Tip:** Tap and hold to edit on your phone or tablet

## Working with the Gradebook

The Gradebook is where you manage grades, grading structures, and track student attendance.

### Opening the Gradebook

1. Go to a course section
2. Click **⋯** → **"Gradebook"**

### The Three Tabs

---

### Tab 1: Categories & Items

**Set up your grading structure here.**

**Left Side - Categories:**

- Create categories like "Assignments", "Exams", "Projects"
- Assign weight to each (must total 100%)
- Choose how to calculate (Equal Weight, Points Based, etc.)

**Right Side - Grade Items:**

- Add specific items like "Midterm Exam" or "Homework 1"
- Set maximum points for each

**Example:**

1. Click **"Add Category"** → Name: "Assignments", Weight: 40%
2. Select the category → Click **"Add Item"** → Name: "Assignment 1", Max: 100 pts
3. Save!

> **Important:** Category weights must total 100%

---

### Tab 2: Grades

**Enter student grades here - works like a spreadsheet!**

- Each row = one student
- Each column = one grade item
- Click a cell, type the grade, press Enter

**The system automatically calculates:**
- Category grades
- Final percentage
- Letter grade

**Don't forget to click "Save"!**

---

### Tab 3: Attendance

**See all your students and track their enrollment status.**

View student names, IDs, emails, majors, and attendance metrics.

**Update Student Status:**

Click the status dropdown to change: Enrolled, Withdrawn, Dropped, or Completed.

---

### Complete Workflow

1. **Categories & Items** → Create categories (weights must = 100%) and add grade items
2. **Grades** → Enter scores, system calculates final grades, click Save
3. **Attendance** → Monitor enrollment and update statuses

## Using SMS on Mobile

SMS works great on your phone or tablet!

### Quick Tips

- **Menu:** Tap the **☰** icon to open/close sidebar
- **Tables:** Swipe left/right to scroll (first column stays visible)
- **Editing:** Tap and hold cells to edit
- **Gradebook:** All three tabs work on mobile

> **Pro Tip:** Use landscape mode for more space when viewing tables!

## Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: Can't log in?**  
A: Check your username and password. Contact IT if you need a reset.

**Q: Grades not saving?**  
A: Make sure category weights add up to 100% and click the Save button.

**Q: Can't see the sidebar on mobile?**  
A: Tap the ☰ menu icon in the top-left corner.

**Q: Changes not appearing?**  
A: Try refreshing the page. Make sure you clicked Save.

### Get Support

Need more help? Contact your institution's IT support team.
